AI Image Cataloger

Drop in a batch of product photos and get each one tagged with its product type, colour and target audience, then sorted into ready-to-download groups. A vision model running on your own machine does the work, so no image ever leaves it.

The problem

Tagging a product catalog by hand is slow and repetitive, and sending thousands of unreleased product photos to a cloud AI service raises cost and privacy questions. This tool does the tagging with a local model instead: private by default and free to run at any volume.

How it works

  1. Drop in a batch

    Drag up to 30 product images into the app at once.

  2. Pick a model

    Choose any vision model installed locally. It defaults to Google's Gemma 3; LLaVA and similar models work too.

  3. Get structured tags

    The model has to answer in a fixed format: product type, product colour, and men's, women's or unisex. No free-form text to clean up.

  4. Download by group

    Images are sorted by colour, audience and product, and each group downloads as its own ZIP.
